The main finding · team test, 15 questions × 5 assistants

Café Athena wins when people ask what it is, and loses when they ask what it offers

Wins

Identity questions like “best Greek restaurant in Pacific Beach.” Most assistants named it; Perplexity almost every time.

Loses

Occasion and feature questions, even though Café Athena offers every one of them.

Why competitors win: assistants quote specific details

  • Athens Market Taverna: “upstairs private dining room for 20–80 guests with a private bar”
  • PB AleHouse: “Brewers Table seats 25,” “private dining for 40–100”
  • Costa Brava: “half-price tapas, $2 sangria, Sun–Thu 4–6 PM”
  • Mezé: “meze happy hour with flaming saganaki, dips, kebabs”

What Claude said about Café Athena: “I don’t have confirmation that it has an enclosed private room, only that it does banquets,” and “vegetarian-friendly with some vegan options.”

Assistants recommend places they can describe with quotable facts. Café Athena’s facts online are vague, so they aren’t confident enough to name it. Its biggest rival is Mezé Greek Fusion, another Greek restaurant that beats it on happy hour, private rooms and catering.
