Greek Mythology Cocktails in Pacific Beach

Our full bar serves signature cocktails named for the gods and heroes of Greek myth. They're a favorite for date night in PB.

The Athena

Gin, lemon, honey, fresh thyme. Our namesake: bright and clever.

Poseidon's Punch

Rum, pineapple, blue curaçao, lime. A nod to the ocean down the street.

Dionysus Spritz

Greek wine, aperitivo, soda, orange. Made for long happy hours.

Apollo's Fire

Mezcal, chili, grapefruit, agave. Smoky and warm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I host a rehearsal dinner at Café Athena?

Yes. Café Athena hosts rehearsal dinners in Pacific Beach for groups of 25 to 60, with a custom family-style Greek menu and bar package.

Does Café Athena have a private room for a party of 25?

Yes. Our semi-private dining area seats up to 25. Call ahead to reserve it.

When is happy hour at Café Athena?

Every day from 3 to 6 PM, with tapas-style Greek small plates and drink specials at 1846 Garnet Ave, Pacific Beach.

Does Café Athena have vegan and gluten-free options?

Yes. The menu marks vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free Greek dishes, including dolmades, skordalia and souvlaki plates.

What are Café Athena's cocktails like?

Our full bar serves signature cocktails inspired by Greek mythology, a popular choice for date night in PB.

Does Café Athena do catering in San Diego?

Yes. We cater Greek and Mediterranean food across San Diego for office lunches, weddings and parties.

How long has Café Athena been open?

Since 1990. We've served Pacific Beach for more than 35 years.
